SCOPE: Final direction round for the app icon — ALL TEAL. Six candidates
varying eye-state and coat pattern. Base everything on the quality level
of the strongest prior refinements. Then STOP for my selection.

SHARED REQUIREMENTS (all six):
- 1024×1024 square PNG, no transparency, no text or lettering.
- A single long-haired cat curled into a circle, fur rendered as hundreds
  of fine individual flowing strands with luminous rim-lighting — the
  betta-fin silk quality. Painterly, rich, silky.
- Palette: teal, cyan, aqua, and soft seafoam gradients only, with deep
  blue-gray shadow tones. No warm colors except where a candidate's spec
  says otherwise.
- THE FACE IS THE HERO: head large (as in the best prior refinements),
  centered or near-centered, with strong tonal contrast against the
  surrounding fur; the face is the brightest focal area.
- Background: clean near-black charcoal ONLY. No sparkles, no stars, no
  particles, no secondary elements.
- Outer glow: soft and fading gently into the dark ground — NEVER a hard
  bright halo ring around the silhouette (a prior round drifted into an
  "eclipse ring"; avoid that failure).
- Perfectly centered, entire curl visible with a small margin.

CANDIDATES:
A — Sleeping teal tabby: eyes fully closed, serene, head resting on the
    curl. The original cozy mood with the improved large face.
B — Slow-blink teal tabby: eyes HALF-closed in the contented cat
    "slow blink," calm and soft — not alert, not asleep. Frontal face.
C — Eyes-open teal tabby: frontal face, gentle relaxed gaze (soft, not
    intense or staring), glowing teal eyes, crisp ears.
D — Sleeping teal calico: calico patch pattern reinterpreted in the teal
    palette — patches of deep teal, pale seafoam/cream, and dark
    blue-gray instead of orange/white/black. Eyes closed, serene.
E — Slow-blink teal calico: same teal-calico patch pattern, eyes
    half-closed in the slow blink. Frontal face.
F — Wildcard: your best interpretation of "calm teal strand-cat" —
    one free swing, same shared requirements.

DELIVERY:
- Deliver the six 1024×1024 master PNG files individually — no collage,
  no downscaling, no compression of the masters.
- Any previews must be Lanczos/bicubic downscales of the masters — never
  regenerated small, never nearest-neighbor.
- Then STOP. No installation, no app.json changes, nothing from Phase 2
  until I approve a final.
