BUG FIX — pet-name/caption overlay position doesn't account for blur-letterboxed 
(crop-contain) posts

CONTEXT: The pet-name/caption overlay (bottom scrim showing pet name, following-
status, breed, "View full photo") was built assuming the old single-rendering-mode 
feed, where the image always fills the tile edge-to-edge (cover-fit). Now that the 
WYSIWYG fix allows posts to render with blur letterboxing (crop-contain — see 
FocalImage.tsx isCropContain branch), the overlay's positioning does not account 
for whether it's landing over actual photo content or over a blurred letterbox pad. 
In a real screenshot, this produced an overlay placement that looked broken/
mistaken — likely landing in the ambiguous zone between real image and blur pad, or 
overlapping content it shouldn't.

GOAL: The pet-name/caption overlay must always anchor sensibly relative to the 
ACTUAL PHOTO CONTENT bounds, not the outer container bounds — regardless of 
whether the post is rendering full-bleed (cover-fit) or letterboxed (crop-contain 
with blur padding).

Specifically:
- For posts rendering with blur letterboxing (crop-contain), determine whether the 
  overlay should sit over the blur pad area (treating it as "safe" negative space, 
  similar to how Instagram/other apps place captions over letterboxed video) or 
  should hug the edge of the actual photo content — pick whichever avoids ever 
  rendering across the photo/blur boundary in a way that looks accidental. Use 
  design judgment here consistent with the existing frosted-scrim/gradient pattern 
  already used elsewhere in the app.
- For posts still rendering full-bleed (cover-fit, no crop rect, or a crop rect 
  that happens to fill the container), preserve exactly the current overlay 
  behavior — no regression for the majority of existing/simple posts.

DO NOT MODIFY:
- The gesture engine, WYSIWYG contain-fit image rendering logic, or the crop rect 
  data model — all confirmed working, do not touch.
- The ratio-label/mode-toggle layout fix from the prior delivery — keep it intact.
- Boop/treat action rail positioning (already patched for crop-contain posts in an 
  earlier delivery) — do not regress that fix.

ACCEPTANCE TEST (must end in the golden path):
1. On an actual mobile device via pshpsh.net: find or create a few posts with 
   different framing — a full-bleed cover-fit post, and a crop-contain post with 
   visible blur letterboxing (e.g. a tall Original-ratio photo posted with padding).
2. Confirm the pet-name/caption overlay looks intentional and legible on EVERY 
   case — never overlapping oddly, never looking like a rendering mistake.
3. Confirm existing full-bleed posts are completely unchanged from current 
   behavior.
4. Golden path: sign in → Home → boop persists → Nursery pager → post flow → sign 
   out.
5. Verify on web, iOS, and Android.
